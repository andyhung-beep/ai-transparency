# AI Transparency and User Trust: Literature Review Wiki


**Summary**: Table of contents for the AI transparency and user trust literature review wiki.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-18

---

This wiki supports a systematic literature review on **AI transparency and user trust**, organized around three core research questions:

1. **RQ1** — How have AI transparency and user trust been conceptualized and operationalized across domains?
2. **RQ2** — What patterns of relationships between transparency and trust have been reported, including non-linear or conditional effects?
3. **RQ3** — How do studies account for user roles or expertise when examining transparency–trust relationships?
