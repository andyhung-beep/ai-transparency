## Key Findings (Summary)

For convenience, the paper's core findings are summarized here. Full detail is in [[tatasciore-loft-time-pressure-transparency]].

- High automation transparency improved accuracy of automation use (sensitivity d') and increased perceived trust and usability in a UV management task (defence domain).
- High time pressure reduced accuracy and increased workload.
- High transparency did not mitigate the negative effects of high time pressure — no transparency × time pressure interaction was found.
- High transparency increased automation bias (criterion c), indicating greater tendency to agree with the Recommender.
- Automation reliability was ~80% (similar to human manual performance); participants used a within-subjects design (N=64 undergraduates).
